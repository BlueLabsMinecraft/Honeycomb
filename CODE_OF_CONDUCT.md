# Code of conduct

## Our standards

Be direct and be kind. Disagree with the idea, not the person. Assume the other person
is trying to help.

Examples of behaviour that keeps this place useful:

* describing a bug with the version, the config and the log line,
* explaining why a design is a bad fit instead of calling it wrong,
* giving credit when someone else's report led to a fix.

Examples of behaviour that is not welcome:

* personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory language,
* publishing someone's private information,
* derailing threads, or repeating a demand that was already answered,
* using issues for support of a fork or a leaked build.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that break these rules, and may
block accounts that repeat them. Report a problem to the maintainers through a private
channel or GitHub's reporting tools.
